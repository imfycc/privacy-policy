# Privacy Policy for LuckyWallet

No data or personal information is collected by this app.

All bills, cards, reward cash records and attachments are processed on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud container and is only accessible to you and the people you invite through iCloud sharing.

##### Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this privacy policy, do not hesitate to [contact me](https://imfy.cc/contact).
